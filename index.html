About

I am a PhD student at the Centre for Digital Music (C4DM), Queen Mary University of London, supervised by Dr. Aidan Hogg. My research interests lie in music and audio, with a focus on expressive performance modelling, analysis and generation.

Previously I received my MEng in Mechanical Engineering from University of Bristol and an MSc in Sound and Music Computing from Queen Mary University of London, where my growing interest in the field led me to continue on to a PhD.
